# PR02 research scope and limitations

Dated 2026-09-12 UTC; business market Dnipro, Ukraine. Ukrainian pages, Russian queries additional research only. Public GET/HEAD, recursive sitemap/anchor/head-feed/media discovery and Google Chrome DOM observations; no forms, appointments, ads clicked or profile changes. Browser samples used hl=uk, gl=ua, pws=0, desktop; sign-in link visible. Google footer used an IP-derived Kyiv location and sometimes a Dnipro/street query context. Precise-location request declined. No geo-grid, neutral-personalization guarantee or numeric ranks.

Twenty exact Google queries were sampled (17 required Ukrainian + 3 Russian representatives), with three leading organic domains transcribed per query. Four retained PR00 Russian formulations remain NOT_SAMPLED. Unknown full Google result URLs are explicitly retained where redirects were opaque/breadcrumbs truncated; exact representative routes were checked on first-party sites. These are current visible result/page-type samples, not proven universal winners. Refresh within 30 days before implementing a brief, or earlier after significant SERP/site change; repeat service/local samples from approved Dnipro coordinates/devices before geographic conclusions.

Eight local packs, four knowledge panels and one DENTIX Maps place detail were separately recorded. Ads are excluded from competitor position evidence. Thirteen AI Overview feature appearances were noticed; only three responses (therapy/wisdom/implantation) received bounded content/citation review. Remaining AIO query responses are not full samples. ChatGPT Search and Bing/Copilot controlled response tests remain ACCESS_BLOCKED; web.run search is supplementary discovery, not an independent AI recommendation answer. Each AI sample is one round of visible captured material, not all expanded sources, a stable rank, referral or business outcome. No patient/reviewer narratives retained.

Crawl includes source HTML metadata/H1/lang, headers/status/redirects, all linked sitemap children, feeds and relevant embedded/linked media variants. Discovery terminates with empty link/sitemap queue; 61 discovered URLs plus 3 explicit probes form 64 inventory/map rows. Nine discovered HTML pages returned 200; seven feeds, seven robots/sitemap endpoints and 38 media URLs account for other discovered URLs. The extra probes are sitemap_index.xml (404), HTTP apex and HTTPS www. The existing /sitemap.xml 302 target is the WordPress index. No /ru/ page expansion; source html lang=ru-RU conflicts with visible Ukrainian core page copy and requires later correction.

Search `site:dentix.ua` surfaced existing home, prosthetics and reviews; no additional path outside crawl inventory. Search results are not exhaustive and are not GSC indexation proof. No standalone emergency URL was found; urgent care/extraction/wisdom services appear on homepage. The default-looking privet-mir slug is titled Comments, so it is held for owner review rather than blindly retired. Public source cannot prove absence of private/dynamic comments, orphan URLs, query aliases, external links or subscriber use. GSC Wizard returned subscription-required; no property data was received. Metrics/backlinks/traffic are UNKNOWN or ACCESS_BLOCKED, never zero. Complete here means discoverable through attempted public channels, not a guarantee against log/GSC-only orphans. Cutover remains blocked until owner resolves these gaps.

Only structured public facts/short metadata and source URLs are distributable. Raw fetched HTML was used locally outside repository and excluded from sanitized ZIP (may contain public user content). All .seo observations are scoped to DENTIX; competitor facts are labelled third-party research, never DENTIX-approved facts. No Google/Maps/AI/lead achievement is asserted; outcome_report.claims=[] and RECORD only validates recorded evidence.
